One task group, one bead. Group 1 is the project contract and the ingest as a library with registration and the collision guard — the store learns projects and nothing draws them yet; group 2 is the board read, the `board` renderer everywhere renderers are named, the procedures, and the route with a placeholder; group 3 is the watcher, the change subscription, and refresh — the server stays current and says so; group 4 is the board component, the live check, and the dogfood. Groups 2 and 3 both depend on 1 and can run in parallel (they meet only in `server/router.ts` and `server/context.ts`); group 4 needs both. Every group ends with `pnpm check` green. Test fixtures are scoped by a unique external-id prefix or source system per test, never a store-wide count (`bd memories test-no-run-opened-assertions`).
